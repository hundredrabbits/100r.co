2026.05.14
Victoria, B.C.

On March 30th we ordered a 105 L high-density polyethylene water tank from Trotac. It took a while for us to get it because fittings are spin-welded on-site and that the person responsible for doing it was out of town for 2 weeks. Why a new water tank? We decided to stop using the 50 L lorry tank we had intalled in the v-berth for water because of weight concerns at the bow, we bought 130 ft of chain last season and the added weight of that plus a full tank is too much. We decided to use the lorry tank as an extra locker for food instead.

The goal is to install the new water tank just aft of the galley, in the starboard-side locker. Pino's cockpit locker is cavernous, fitting a big tank in there would be easy. While waiting for the tank, we decided to take one of the galley cabinets apart to redo it because the back was all worn and rotten.

Taking the old cabinet apart was easy, most of the finishing nails were rusty. Our plan was to rebuild the cabinet without buying new wood. Our friend Peter graciously donated a short length of marine ply so we could replace the backside, we used bits of wood we already had on hand to make up the rest (we had to glue and bind some bits together to form some of the walls, it worked out well in the end).
